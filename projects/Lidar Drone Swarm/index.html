Project Summary

A swarm of autonomous quadcopters that map buildings with lidar and cameras for structural inspection.

Project Details

  • Swarm of quadcopters autonomously navigate and create lidar and camera scans of structures.
  • FAST-LIO SLAM algorithm used for localization in unison with a frontier exploration scheme.
  • Custom flight controller(see related project) used for flight control with RP-CM5 for compute, lidar, and camera.
  • Avoids collisions using MIT's MADER to reroute trajectories if needed.
  • Combines lidar point clouds and camera scans from different angles into one large scan.

Project Description

Overview

This is a swarm of drones I helped build as part of a small team. Each drone carries a lidar and digital and IR cameras to map buildings for inspection. The drones talk to each other over a local Wi-Fi network to avoid collisions, and they combine their maps to scan large structures efficiently.

Autonomy

  • Localization: FAST-LIO lidar-inertial SLAM.
  • Exploration: a frontier exploration scheme decides where each drone scans next.
  • Collision avoidance: MIT's MADER planner reroutes trajectories between drones when needed.
  • Mapping: lidar point clouds and camera scans from different angles are merged into one large scan.

Hardware

Each drone flies on my custom flight controller, with an RP-CM5 handling compute, the lidar and the cameras.
