Project Summary

Custom Avionics for Amateur Rocketry. Flight computers with sensing, data logging, telemetry, motor control, and ignition capabilities.

Project Details

  • A family of custom flight computer PCBs and firmware.
  • Based on the RP2350 family of microcontrollers.
  • Built in 9 axis motion, GPS, and barometric sensing.
  • Motor control and ignition capabilities.
