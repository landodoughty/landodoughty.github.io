Project Summary

A fully 3D-printed, modular RC hovercraft that travels over land and water.

Project Details

  • Dual BLDC motor system with separate lift and thrust control.
  • Fully 3D-printed frame needs no supports for efficient printing.
  • Uses M3 hardware and heat-set inserts for fast and strong assembly.
  • Servo-controlled rudder for precise directional movement.
  • Lightweight air skirt constructed from a standard trash bag.

Project Description

Overview

This RC hovercraft is a fully 3D-printed, modular vehicle built to travel over land and water. Two brushless DC motors split the work: one provides lift and the other drives forward thrust. A servo-controlled rudder steers the craft.

Design

The lift motor blows air into a skirt made from a household trash bag, so the craft floats on a cushion of air. The thrust motor pushes air past the rudder, which steers the hovercraft on any surface.

Build

The frame prints without supports. Parts join with M3 hardware and heat-set inserts, which makes the hovercraft strong, quick to assemble and easy to repair or modify. The 3D models are free on Printables.
